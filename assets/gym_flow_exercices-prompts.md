# Gym Flow - Image Generation Prompts

Este archivo contiene prompts detallados para generar imágenes de ejercicios con el estilo visual exacto de la referencia: personaje gris, fondo blanco, y músculos resaltados en rojo.

## Barbell Curl (Curl con barra)check
- **Categoría:** arms
- **Músculos:** biceps
- **Equipo:** barbell

```
**Prompt:** A detailed medical-anatomical illustration of a human male figure performing a Barbell Curl. 
- **Composition:** Two side-by-side panels. Left panel shows the 'START' position. Right panel shows the 'FINISH' position.
- **Style:** Clean minimalist vector art, technical illustration, white background.
- **Character:** Athletic male figure with light gray skin (#D3D3D3), white glowing eyes, and thick black outlines. Simplified facial features.
- **Equipment:** barbell shown in dark gray/black.
- **Coloring:** The biceps must be highlighted in solid, vibrant red (#FF0000) in both panels. All other body parts remain gray.
- **Details:** Ensure the anatomical structure and movement of the Barbell Curl are accurate. No text, no background shadows.
```
