Conventional Deadlift

accessibility_new Posterior Chain
fitness_center Barbell

Execution

01

Stand with your mid-foot under the barbell. Your shins should not be touching the bar yet. Point your toes slightly outward.

02

Bend over and grab the bar with a shoulder-width grip. Keep your arms straight and do not drop your hips.

03

Bend your knees until your shins touch the bar. Do not let the bar roll forward away from your mid-foot.

04

Lift your chest up to straighten your lower back. Pull the slack out of the bar and brace your core tightly.

05

Drive through the floor with your legs to stand up with the weight. Lock out your hips and knees simultaneously at the top.
